## Station: Lesen und Verstehen | Text, Bild, Infokästen, Tabelle
{tippen} Last Saturday Emma **visited** London with her family. They **took** the train and **saw** Big Ben.

![Emma's trip to London | klein](anhang:london)

[Merke] The simple past is for finished actions in the past: *yesterday, last week, two days ago*.

[Regel] Regular verbs: verb + **-ed** (play → played). Irregular verbs: learn them by heart (go → went).

[Tipp] Signal words help you: *yesterday, last …, … ago, in 2020*.

[Achtung] After **did** or **didn't** the verb stays in the base form: *Did you go?* – not *Did you went?*

[Begriff: irregular verb] A verb that does not form the past with -ed, e.g. *see → saw*.

[Figur: Aha] Wow, Emma was really busy in London!

| base form | simple past |
| --- | --- |
| go | went |
| see | saw |
| take | took |

### Aufdecken: Did you know?
Big Ben is the name of the big bell – not of the tower.

## Station: Wörter und Formen | Markieren, Sortieren, Zuordnen
### Markieren: Mark all verbs in the simple past.
Yesterday Emma {{woke}} up early. She {{had}} breakfast and {{walked}} to the station. Today she is tired and wants to sleep.

### Sortieren: Sort the verbs.
regular verbs: played, visited, walked
irregular verbs: went, saw, took

### Zuordnen: Match the base form and the simple past.
go -> went
see -> saw
buy -> bought
eat -> ate

## Station: Sätze bauen | Lückentext, Satzpuzzle, Reihenfolge
### Lückentext: Fill in the simple past.
Yesterday I [[watched]] (watch) a film. Then we [[went]] (go) for a pizza.

### Lückentext: Choose the right word.
Emma [[visited]] London last year. She [[didn't]] see the King.
Wortbank: ja
Extra: visits, don't

### Satzpuzzle: Put the words in the right order.
did / you / visit / London / last summer
Auch richtig: last summer / did / you / visit / London

### Reihenfolge: Put Emma's day in the right order.
- In the morning Emma took the train to London.
- At noon she saw Big Ben.
- In the afternoon she visited a museum.
- In the evening she went back home.

## Station: Zeig, was du kannst | Quiz, Aufgabe, Sperre
### Quiz
? Which sentence is correct?
[x] She went home. // Right: go → went.
[ ] She goed home. // Careful: go is an irregular verb.
[ ] She go home yesterday. // You need the past form here.
? What is the simple past of *buy*?
[ ] buyed // buy is irregular.
[x] bought // Yes!
[ ] brought // That is the past of *bring*.

### Aufgabe: Your turn
Write the simple past of **take**.
Antwort: took
Tipp: It rhymes with *book*.
Lösung: take → took
